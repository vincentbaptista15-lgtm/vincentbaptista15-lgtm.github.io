PINoy KITCHEN WEBSITE

Files:
- index.html: page structure
- style.css: layout, colors, and responsive design
- script.js: recipe data, search, filters, random dish, recipe detail, cooking steps, tips, and theme toggle
- images/: optional folder for local food photos

HOW TO OPEN:
1. Extract the ZIP folder.
2. Open the PinoyFoodWebsite folder.
3. Double-click index.html, or right-click it and choose Open with > Chrome.
4. An internet connection is needed for the online food photos and Google Fonts.
5. Use the Dark mode button in the navigation to switch themes. Your choice is saved in this browser.
6. Browse recipes by course using the Appetizers, Breakfast, Lunch, and Dinner filters. Breakfast, Lunch, and Dinner recipes are also displayed in separate sections.

HOW TO USE YOUR OWN PHOTOS:
Put your photo files in images/, then edit the image property in script.js.
For example, change:
image: "https://images.unsplash.com/..."
to:
image: "images/adobo.jpg"

Note: Food photos currently use online image URLs, so the images folder is provided for your own local photos.
